COUNCIL STATE

A simulation of life, money and power in 90's Britain.

Start with nothing. Grind your way up on the straight and narrow, or cross the line. Build legitimacy in the business world, or fear on the streets. Influence enough people and watch towns, cities and counties shift beneath your decisions.

-

Power structures rise and fall. Every connection made. Every deal struck. Every obstacle cleared by whatever means, forms a part of your story and how the world reacts to you.
